# 🚀 CodePiece: A code-learning Android Application using Kotlin

Welcome to **CodePiece**, the ultimate Android app crafted for coding beginners and students! Take your first steps into the world of programming with a vibrant, interactive, and resource-rich platform—right from your Android device.

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

### 🌐 **Learn Multiple Languages**
- **C, C++, Java, and Python** — All in one place!
- Every code snippet is available in all four languages, letting you compare and understand syntax side-by-side.

### 🏆 **Interactive Quizzes & Progress Tracking**
- Challenge yourself with quizzes covering various programming topics.
- Instantly see your results and monitor your progress over time.

### 📚 **Downloadable Code Books**
- Access and download essential programming books directly within the app.
- Build your offline coding library with handpicked resources.

### 🎥 **YouTube Video Lessons**
- Watch high-quality, curated YouTube tutorials without leaving the app.
- Learn from the best: from basics to advanced topics—video learning is just a tap away!

### 🔐 **Admin Dashboard**
- **Admin Login:** Special administrative access for managing the app content.
- **Dynamic Content Management:** Upload new problem statements, quiz questions, books, and courses to keep the app fresh and up-to-date.

---

## 👩‍💻 Who Should Use CodePiece?

- **Absolute Beginners:** Start learning programming from scratch.
- **Students:** Prepare for exams, assignments, and interviews.
- **Self-learners:** Explore new languages and topics at your own pace.
- **Teachers:** Share resources and track student progress.

## 🎯 Why Choose CodePiece?

- **All-in-One:** Learn, practice, and assess yourself—no need for multiple apps!
- **Multi-language Support:** Seamlessly switch between C, C++, Java, and Python.
- **Up-to-Date Content:** Regularly updated quizzes, books, and tutorials.
- **Offline Friendly:** Download books and read them anytime, anywhere.
- **Easy Admin Tools:** Effortlessly expand or update the app’s learning materials.

## 🚦 Getting Started

1. **Install CodePiece** on your Android device.
2. **Explore** all features and enhance your coding knowledge.
3. **Admins:** Log in to manage and curate the learning content for all users.
